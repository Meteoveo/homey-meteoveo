MeteoVeo is a weather service for Spain built on measurements: radar, thousands of nearby weather stations and AEMET, combined into a forecast for the next minutes and hours at your home.

With this app your Homey knows:
- whether rain is coming in the next hour, and when it starts and stops;
- the current temperature, humidity, pressure, wind and UV index at home, with today's and tomorrow's maximum and minimum;
- active AEMET weather warnings for your area.

Use it in Flows: retract the awning before the rain, skip the garden watering, or get a notification with the radar image when an orange warning is issued. Two dashboard widgets come with it: the forecast in the look of the MeteoVeo app, with the next hour of rain, our own 3-hour forecast and 7 days, and a radar loop of the last two hours around your home.

Setting up takes a minute: create a free MeteoVeo account (or sign in) while adding the device, confirm the code we e-mail you, and pick your location. No API keys to copy.

Own a weather station? Choose its devices in Homey, or send readings with Flows, and they become your station at MeteoVeo. Your readings make the forecast for your home more accurate.

Coverage: mainland Spain, the Balearic and Canary Islands, Ceuta and Melilla.

Removing the device only disconnects this Homey. To delete your MeteoVeo account, open the device and choose Repair.

Ideas for your Flows:
- Awning: when rain is on its way, or gusts above 40 km/h are expected within 1 hour, retract the awning.
- Sun and heat: if the sun is shining and today's maximum is above 30 °C, lower the awning or the blinds at midday.
- Watering: before watering, if today's chance of rain is above 60 %, skip it.
- Warnings: when a warning is issued, send a notification with the radar image.
- Fresh air: in the evening, if it is at least 2 °C cooler outside than inside, open the windows instead of running the air conditioning.
- Tropical nights: if tonight will be a tropical night, schedule the bedroom air conditioning.

Orange and red AEMET warnings reach the Homey timeline without any Flow; change that in the device settings. Tip: to keep the outdoor temperature out of your home's climate, switch on Exclude from Climate in the device's Advanced Settings.

To change the location, sign in again or delete your account: the device's settings, Maintenance, Repair.
