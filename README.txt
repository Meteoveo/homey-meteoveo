Know about the rain before it arrives. MeteoVeo is a weather service for Spain built on measurements from radar, thousands of nearby weather stations and AEMET, and this app brings it to your Homey: rain in the next hour in 10-minute steps, AEMET warnings for your area and the conditions at your home, ready to retract the awning, skip the watering or close the blinds with your Flows. Two dashboard widgets show the forecast in the look of the MeteoVeo app and a radar loop around your home. Available for mainland Spain, the Balearic and Canary Islands, Ceuta and Melilla.

The app is free and works with a free MeteoVeo account. Lightning near your home is part of MeteoVeo Plus, MeteoVeo's paid plan (€1.99 a month or €14.99 a year, with a free trial), which you can get in the MeteoVeo app for Android and iPhone. Own a weather station? Send its readings to MeteoVeo with Flows: they make your forecast more accurate, and for as long as your station keeps reporting, MeteoVeo Plus is free for you.
